# SARITA PUJARI — Editorial Fashion Portfolio

A refined, personal fashion design and garment making portfolio website for **Sarita Pujari** (Fashion Creative & Garment Maker based in Mumbai).

Conceived and designed as an **independent fashion designer's personal visual archive** — quiet, editorial, tactile, and image-led.

---

## ✦ Three Primary Sections

1. **ABOUT (Hero + Personal Story)**:
   - Asymmetric, natural editorial opening featuring Sarita's authentic studio portrait (`/portfolio/profile/sarita-pujari.png`).
   - Opening personal statement:
     - *"Clothes, craft, curiosity, and the things I make along the way."*
     - *"I work with fabric, ideas, silhouettes and the occasional experiment that turns into something worth wearing."*
   - Intimate, authentic biography detailing her roots observing her mother stitch at home, early school embroidery curiosity, co-founding Kifayat Co. with three friends in Mumbai on the clothing rework side, and cultivating her independent practice in garment making, textile exploration, custom fit, and hands-on construction.
   - Genuine workshop photos of sewing and material exploration.

2. **WORK (The Main Portfolio Showcase)**:
   - The heart of the website: 36 total projects across 4 garment silhouettes plus integrated collaborative reworks and textile lifestyle objects.
   - Minimalist category filtering:
     - `All Work (36)`
     - `Tops (19)`
     - `Bottoms (6)`
     - `Dresses (4)`
     - `Co-ord Sets (5)`
     - `Collaborative & Objects (2)` (Kifayat Co. thrift & rework collective, and zero-waste textile lifestyle objects from remnant offcuts)
   - Dynamic asymmetric editorial grid: variable widths, mixed proportions, and natural spacing.
   - Interactive Lightbox:
     - Full high-resolution multi-angle view
     - Angle navigation with previous / next controls
     - Keyboard arrow keys (`←` / `→`) & `Escape` key support
     - Technical design study sheet viewer
     - Touch/swipe friendly on mobile

3. **CONTACT ("Have something in mind?")**:
   - Heading: *"Have something in mind?"*
   - Copy: *"I'm always interested in thoughtful collaborations, interesting materials, new ideas and projects that give me something to explore."*
   - Direct studio email with one-click clipboard copy.
   - Studio location in Mumbai, India.
   - Direct personal inquiry message form.
   - Minimalist footer with smooth back-to-top interaction.

---

## ✦ Tech Stack & Performance

- **Framework**: React 19 + TypeScript
- **Bundler**: Vite 8
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS v4 (`@tailwindcss/vite`)
- **Animation**: `motion/react` (restrained, gentle reveals, respecting `prefers-reduced-motion`)
- **Typography**: Cormorant Garamond & Playfair Display (Editorial Display Serif) + DM Sans (Clean Modern Sans)
- **Palette**: Warm off-white paper (`#F8F5EE`), Ink (`#1C1B18`), Muted Ink (`#6E6961`), Muted Rust (`#8E3B2D`), Soft Sand (`#E2D8C9`)
